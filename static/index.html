EXPERIMENT DESIGN

Methodology

Two image-classification tasks, two model variants, and controlled adversarial perturbations.

01 / DATA

MNIST + GTSRB

MNIST is a simple digit benchmark; GTSRB adds 43 traffic-sign classes relevant to visual-perception research.

02 / ATTACK

FGSM + PGD

FGSM performs one gradient-sign update. PGD performs iterative updates constrained to an epsilon region.

x_adv = clip(x + ε · sign(∇x L))
03 / DEFENSE

Adversarial training

The defended model sees clean and FGSM-perturbed examples during training. It offers no universal robustness guarantee.

04 / EVALUATION

Measure trade-offs

Report clean accuracy, FGSM/PGD accuracy, attack success rate and training time.

Research limitationThis is a seminar prototype, not certified vehicle safety software. Digital attacks do not prove physical-world vulnerability.
